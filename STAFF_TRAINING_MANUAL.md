## 1. Introduction

### What is Quick Wing?

Quick Wing is our fleet management system that helps you:
- ✅ Update vehicle status in seconds using QR codes
- ✅ Check which vehicles are available
- ✅ Book vehicles for specific dates and times
- ✅ Request lifts from colleagues
- ✅ Access breakdown assistance contacts
- ✅ Track vehicle locations

### Who Can Use Quick Wing?

All staff members with login credentials can access Quick Wing.

## 3. Updating Vehicle Status (QR Code)

### Step-by-Step Guide

**Method 1: Scan QR Code (Recommended)**

1. **Find the QR code** inside the vehicle (usually on windscreen or dashboard)

2. **Scan with your phone:**
   - Open your phone's camera app
   - Point at the QR code
   - Tap the notification that appears

3. **Update Status Form Opens:**
   - Vehicle name and registration shown at top
   - You'll see 4 fields:

4. **Select Status (Required):**
   - **Free** - Vehicle is available and parked
   - **In Use** - You're currently using the vehicle
   - **Needs Cleaning** - Vehicle requires interior cleaning
   - **Needs Repair** - Vehicle has mechanical issues

5. **Enter Your Name (Optional but Recommended):**
   - Example: "John Murphy"
   - Helps track who made changes

6. **Add Current Location (Optional but Important):**
   - Example: "Main Office Car Park, Tralee"
   - Example: "N22 Road near Killarney"
   - Example: "Dingle Service Station"
   - Be specific so others can find the vehicle

7. **Add Notes (Optional):**
   - Example: "Low on fuel"
   - Example: "Left at service center"
   - Example: "Keys in reception"

8. **Click "Update Status"**

9. **Success Message:**
   - Green checkmark appears
   - Status updated successfully
   - Takes less than 5 seconds!

### When to Update Status

**Setting Off:**
- Scan QR code
- Change to "In Use"
- Add your location

**Returning:**
- Scan QR code
- Change to "Free"
- Add where you've parked
- Note any issues

**Issues Found:**
- Change to "Needs Cleaning" or "Needs Repair"
- Describe the issue in notes
- Add your name

### Status Colors

- 🟢 **Green = Free** (Available)
- 🔴 **Red = In Use** (Someone using it)
- 🟠 **Orange = Needs Attention** (Cleaning or Repair)

## 5. Making Bookings

### Why Book Vehicles?

- Reserve a vehicle for future use
- Prevents double-booking
- Others can see when you need it
- Helps with planning

### Step-by-Step Booking

1. **Go to Bookings Page**
   - Click 📅 Bookings in navigation

2. **Click "New Booking" Button**

3. **Fill in Booking Form:**

   **Select Car (Required):**
   - Choose from dropdown list
   - Shows vehicle name and registration

   **Your Name (Required):**
   - Enter your full name

   **Start Time (Required):**
   - Click the date/time picker
   - Select when you need the vehicle

   **End Time (Required):**
   - When you'll return it
   - Must be after start time

   **Destination/Notes:**
   - Where you're going
   - Example: "Client meeting in Cork"
   - Example: "Delivery to Killarney"

4. **Click "Create Booking"**

5. **System Checks:**
   - ✅ If available: Booking created
   - ❌ If conflict: Error message shown
   - Conflicts mean someone else already booked that time

### Viewing Your Bookings

**On Bookings Page:**
- See all bookings (yours and others)
- Sorted by date (newest first)
- Shows:
  - Vehicle booked
  - Who booked it
  - Start and end times
  - Destination

### Cancelling a Booking

1. Find your booking in the list
2. Click the 🗑️ Delete icon
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Booking removed
5. Vehicle becomes available again

### Booking Best Practices

**Do:**
- ✅ Book as soon as you know you need a vehicle
- ✅ Be accurate with times
- ✅ Cancel if plans change
- ✅ Add destination info

**Don't:**
- ❌ Book "just in case" - others might need it
- ❌ Forget to cancel if plans change
- ❌ Book multiple vehicles for same time
- ❌ Leave vague time ranges

## 6. Request a Lift Feature

### What is Request a Lift?

This feature allows you to ask colleagues for a ride when you need transportation.

### How to Request a Lift

1. **On the Dashboard**, click the big blue **"🙋‍♂️ Request a Lift"** button at the bottom

2. **Fill in the Request Form:**
   - **Who needs the lift?** - Your name
   - **From where?** - Pickup location
   - **To where?** - Drop-off location
   - **Date** - When you need the lift
   - **Time** - What time
   - **Notes** (optional) - Any extra info

3. **Click "Request Lift"**

4. **Your request appears** on everyone's dashboard

### Viewing Lift Requests

- **Blue panel** at the top of Dashboard shows active requests
- Click the **minimize button (−)** to collapse/expand
- See who needs a lift, where, and when

### Responding to Requests

When you see someone else's lift request:

1. **Click "I can help!"** button
2. **Add a message** for the requester (e.g., "I'll pick you up at the main entrance")
3. **Click "Accept & Send"**

### What Happens After Acceptance

- The **request is removed** from all dashboards
- The **requester gets a notification** showing:
  - Who accepted their request
  - Your message
  - "Got it, thanks!" button to acknowledge

### Managing Your Requests

- **Hide** - Click the eye icon to hide a request from your view only
- **Your requests** show a "You" tag
- Only **admins can delete** requests permanently

### Notification Bell 🔔

- In the top navigation, the **bell icon** shows lift request count
- Click to see a **dropdown** of all active requests
- Quick way to view and respond to requests

## 7. Accessing Breakdown Assistance

### When to Use

- Vehicle breaks down
- Mechanical issues while driving
- Need towing service
- Require urgent maintenance

### How to Access

1. **Go to Assistance Page**
   - Click 📞 Assistance in navigation

2. **Select Region:**
   - Kerry
   - West Cork
   - (Filters providers automatically)

3. **View Provider List:**
   - Company name
   - Service type (Breakdown, Towing, Maintenance)
   - Phone number

4. **Call for Help:**
   - Tap phone number on mobile (calls directly)
   - Or click to copy and dial
   - Or manually dial the number

### Provider Categories

**Breakdown Services:**
- Emergency roadside assistance
- Jump starts
- Flat tire changes
- Basic repairs

**Towing Services:**
- Vehicle recovery
- Transport to garage
- Long-distance towing

**Maintenance Services:**
- Scheduled services
- Repairs
- Parts replacement

### What to Tell Provider

When calling:
1. Your name and company (mention Quick Wing fleet)
2. Vehicle registration number
3. Exact location
4. Nature of problem
5. Whether passengers need assistance

### After Getting Help

**Update Vehicle Status:**
1. Scan QR code or log into app
2. Change status to "Needs Repair"
3. Add notes: "At [Garage Name] for [Issue]"
4. Add your name
5. Include expected return date if known

**Inform Admin:**
- Let admin know about the incident
- Provide repair details
- Keep receipts if applicable

## 9. Troubleshooting

### Can't Login

**Problem:** "Incorrect email or password"

**Solutions:**
- Check email spelling (case sensitive)
- Verify password (check caps lock)
- Contact admin to reset password

**Problem:** "User account is inactive"

**Solution:**
- Your account may be deactivated
- Contact admin to reactivate

### QR Code Not Working

**Problem:** QR code won't scan

**Solutions:**
1. Clean the QR code surface
2. Ensure good lighting
3. Hold phone steady
4. Try moving closer/further
5. Use a QR scanner app if camera doesn't work
6. Manually access: Login → Find vehicle in dashboard

**Problem:** "Car not found" after scanning

**Solution:**
- QR code may be damaged
- Report to admin
- Use app to update manually

### Booking Conflicts

**Problem:** "Booking conflict: Car already booked"

**Solutions:**
1. Check the exact times
2. Choose different times
3. Select another vehicle
4. Contact person who has booking
5. Speak with admin if urgent

### Status Not Updating

**Problem:** Changed status but still shows old status

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 30 seconds for auto-refresh
2. Click "Refresh" button manually
3. Check your internet connection
4. Try updating again
5. Log out and back in

### App Running Slow

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Close and reopen browser
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try on different device
5. Contact admin if persists

### Forgot Password

**Solution:**
1. Contact admin
2. Admin will send new invite link
3. Register with new password
4. Save password securely
