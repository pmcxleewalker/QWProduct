## Introduction

The **Quick Wing Content Worker** is a complete social media management module designed specifically for Instagram content creation and publishing. It provides:

- **Privacy-First Workflow**: Automatically detect and blur sensitive information
- **Brand Consistency**: Generate branded previews with Quick Wing styling
- **AI-Powered Captions**: Get professional caption suggestions
- **Approval Workflow**: Review and approve content before publishing
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Track post performance
- **Content Calendar**: Schedule and manage your posting schedule

### Who Can Access Content Worker?

- **Super Admin**: Full access to all features
- **Social Media Manager**: Full access to content creation and publishing

## Asset Management

Assets are the images and videos you'll use in your Instagram posts.

### Uploading Assets

1. Click **"Assets"** tab
2. Click **"Upload"** button
3. Select your file(s)
   - **Supported Images**: PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF
   - **Supported Videos**: MP4, MOV, WEBM
   - **Max Size**: 100MB per file

### Managing Assets

- **Search**: Find assets by filename
- **Filter**: Filter by type (All / Images / Videos)
- **Preview**: Click any asset to see full-size preview
- **Create Post**: Click "Create Post" on any asset to start a new draft

### Tips for Assets

- Use high-quality images (minimum 1080x1080 for Instagram)
- Keep videos under 60 seconds for Reels
- Name files descriptively for easy searching

## Creating New Posts

The Content Worker uses a **6-step guided workflow** to ensure quality and privacy compliance.

### Step 1: Upload Media

**Choose your media source:**

1. **Select from Library**: Click an existing asset
2. **Upload New**: Drag & drop or click to upload

The selected media will display in the preview area.

### Step 2: Select Frame (Videos Only)

If you uploaded a video:

1. The system extracts 3 key frames automatically
2. Click on a frame thumbnail to select it
3. This frame becomes your post's cover image

*Skip this step for images.*

### Step 3: Privacy Review

**IMPORTANT**: This step protects sensitive information!

The system automatically scans for:
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Names
- Vehicle registration numbers
- Booking details
- Dates and times

**What You'll See:**

1. **Detected Privacy Zones**: Highlighted areas with sensitive info
2. **Zone Types**: Color-coded by type (red=email, orange=phone, etc.)

**Actions You Can Take:**

- **Add Blur Zone**: Click and drag to add manual blur areas
- **Remove Zone**: Click X on any zone to remove it
- **Toggle Preview**: Switch between Original and Blurred views
- **Mark as Reviewed**: Confirm you've checked all sensitive content

### Step 4: Generate Preview

Create branded Instagram-ready images:

1. Click **"Generate Preview"**
2. Two formats are created:
   - **Square** (1080x1080) - For feed posts
   - **Portrait** (1080x1350) - For Reels/Stories

Previews include:
- Your blur zones applied
- Quick Wing branding bar at bottom
- Professional framing

### Step 5: Generate Captions

Get AI-powered caption suggestions:

1. **Select Content Focus**:
   - Booking Simplicity
   - Calendar Visibility
   - Compliance Tracking
   - Admin Efficiency
   - Time Saving
   - Reducing Chaos
   - Operational Clarity

2. Click **"Generate Captions"**

3. **Review 3 Caption Options**:
   - Each includes: Hook, Body, CTA, Hashtags
   - Click to select your preferred option
   - Edit if needed

### Step 6: Save Draft

1. Review all your selections
2. Add **Internal Notes** (not published - for team reference)
3. Choose action:
   - **Save as Draft**: Save for later editing
   - **Submit for Review**: Send for approval

## Review & Approval Process

The Review Queue ensures content quality before publishing.

### Accessing Review Queue

1. Click **"Review Queue"** tab
2. Use the **Status Filter** dropdown to filter:
   - All Status
   - Drafts
   - In Review
   - Approved
   - Scheduled
   - Publishing
   - Posted
   - Failed
   - Rejected

### Reviewing a Draft

1. Click the **eye icon** on any draft to preview
2. Review the following:
   - **Original vs Processed** image toggle
   - **Privacy Zones** summary
   - **Caption Options**
   - **Draft Details** (creator, date, content focus)

### Approval Actions

| Action | When to Use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Approve** | Content is ready to publish |
| **Reject** | Content doesn't meet standards (add notes explaining why) |
| **Send Back to Draft** | Minor changes needed, creator should revise |

### After Approval

Once approved, the draft moves to "Ready to Post" status and can be:
- Published immediately
- Scheduled for a specific date/time

## Scheduling & Publishing

### From the Review Queue

For **Approved** drafts:

1. Click **"Schedule"** button
2. Select date and time
3. Click **"Schedule Post"**

Or click **"Publish Now"** to post immediately.

### From the Posted Content Calendar

Access via **"Posted"** tab.

#### Four Status Views

| Tab | Content Shown |
|-----|---------------|
| **Posted** | Successfully published content |
| **Scheduled** | Content scheduled for future |
| **Ready to Post** | Approved content awaiting action |
| **Failed** | Publishing errors |

#### Actions by Status

**Ready to Post:**
- Schedule with datetime picker
- "Publish to Instagram" button

**Scheduled:**
- "Unschedule" - Revert to Ready to Post
- "Publish Now" - Publish immediately

**Posted:**
- "View on Instagram" - Open post on Instagram
- "View Analytics" - See performance metrics

**Failed:**
- View error message
- "Retry Publish" button

### Instagram Connection Required

You must connect your Instagram account before publishing.

Look for the connection status in the header:
- **Green checkmark**: "Instagram Connected" - Ready to publish
- **Orange warning**: "Connect Instagram" - Setup required

## Content Analytics

Track how your content performs on Instagram.

### Accessing Analytics

Click **"Analytics"** tab in Content Worker.

### Overview Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Likes** | Sum of all likes across posts |
| **Total Comments** | Sum of all comments |
| **Total Reach** | Number of unique accounts reached |
| **Total Saves** | Times users saved your posts |

1. Click **"Sync Metrics"** button (purple gradient)
2. Latest performance data is pulled from Instagram
3. All metrics update automatically

### Performance Insights

**Top Posts Section:**
- Ranked by selected metric (Likes, Comments, Reach, Saves)
- Shows thumbnail, post title, and key metrics
- Click external link to view on Instagram

**Recent Posts Section:**
- Chronologically sorted
- Shows publish date and like count

**Performance by Category:**
- Which content types perform best
- Average likes and engagement per category

**Performance by Format:**
- Reels vs Carousels vs Single Images vs Stories
- Average reach and likes per format

## Settings & Instagram Connection

### Accessing Settings

Click **"Settings"** tab in Content Worker.

### Instagram Connection

**To Connect:**

1. Click **"Connect Account"** button
2. Enter your Instagram credentials:
   - **Account Name**: Your Instagram handle
   - **Access Token**: From Meta Developer Portal
   - **Account ID**: (Optional) Instagram Business Account ID
3. Click **"Connect"**

**Connection Details:**

Once connected, you'll see:
- Account name (@youraccount)
- Token Status (Valid for X days)
- Last Sync timestamp
- Connected date

**Token Refresh:**

If token is expiring soon:
1. Warning banner appears
2. Click **"Refresh Token"**
3. Follow Meta's re-authentication flow

**To Disconnect:**

1. Click **"Disconnect"** button
2. Confirm disconnection
3. Publishing will be disabled until reconnected

### Post Templates

Create reusable content templates:

1. Click **"Add Template"**
2. Configure:
   - Template Name
   - Format Type (Reel, Carousel, Single Image, Story)
   - Brand Style
   - Logo Position
3. Save template

### Publishing Settings

- **Require Approval**: All posts need approval (always on)
- **Privacy Review Required**: Prompt for privacy check (recommended)

## Best Practices

### Content Creation

1. **Use High-Quality Media**
   - Minimum 1080x1080 resolution
   - Good lighting and clear subjects
   - Relevant to fleet management

2. **Always Review Privacy**
   - Check all detected zones
   - Manually add blur for anything missed
   - Don't skip this step!

3. **Choose Appropriate Captions**
   - Match caption tone to your audience
   - Include clear CTAs
   - Use relevant hashtags

### Publishing Schedule

1. **Plan Ahead**
   - Schedule posts for optimal times
   - Maintain consistent posting frequency
   - Use the Content Calendar view

2. **Monitor Performance**
   - Sync metrics regularly
   - Adjust strategy based on analytics
   - Double down on what works

### Team Workflow

1. **Use Internal Notes**
   - Document reasons for content decisions
   - Leave instructions for reviewers
   - Track revisions

2. **Provide Clear Feedback**
   - When rejecting, explain why
   - Use "Send Back to Draft" for minor changes
   - Be specific about required edits

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"Instagram not connected" error when publishing**
- Go to Settings tab
- Click "Connect Account"
- Enter valid credentials

**Token expired**
- Go to Settings tab
- Click "Refresh Token"
- Re-authenticate with Instagram

**Privacy zones not detected**
- OCR may miss stylized text
- Manually add blur zones for missed areas
- Mark as reviewed when complete

**Preview generation failed**
- Check image file is valid
- Try re-uploading the asset
- Ensure file size under 100MB

**Publish failed**
- Check Instagram connection status
- Verify account has posting permissions
- Click "Retry Publish" after fixing issues
